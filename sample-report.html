What this report trusted

729 rows read. 14 were shorter than 2 minutes and were dropped as accidental starts. They matter more than their count suggests: a four-second phantom run makes a long layoff look months shorter than it was.

Heart rate quarantine. 155 sustained sessions were logged with an average heart rate under 95 bpm, which is a strap or pairing failure rather than an easy day. 2024 is excluded from efficiency trends, and load for those sessions was scored from duration and training effect instead.

How load was scored. 518 sessions from heart rate, using Banister's TRIMP. The other 197 had no usable heart rate, so they were scored as a typical session of that sport for you, measured from your own clean data. Aerobic TE was not used to fill the gap: Garmin computes it from heart rate, so where the strap failed it failed too. Scoring those months near zero would invent a fitness collapse that never happened.

Heart rate bounds. Max 183, resting 52, with the max taken from the 98th percentile of your recorded peaks rather than the single highest beat. Pass --max-hr if you know it from a test.
